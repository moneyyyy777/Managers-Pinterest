Why It’s Harder to Build Muscle After 40

Many people notice it gets harder to stay strong as they get older. The reason is a natural enzyme in your body called mTOR. This enzyme acts like a switch for muscle growth.

After 40, this switch does not work as it used to. As a result, your body struggles to use protein, rebuild muscle, and recover from exercise. That’s why diet and exercise alone don’t work like they once did.
